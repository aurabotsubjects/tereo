Pronunciation guide

a = “ah” (car) · e = “eh” (bed) · i = “ee” (see) · o = “or” (door, short) · u = “oo” (moon)

A macron (ā ē ī ō ū) means hold the vowel longer. wh sounds like a soft “f”. ng is as in “singer”, not “finger”.
